📖 How To Play Aetheria (Guide & Controls)

🎮 Keyboard Controls & Keys

Key / Shortcut Action Description
W A S D or Arrow Keys Movement Move your hero around the dungeon map.
SPACE Melee Sword Attack Swings your sword to hit nearby enemies.
1 Fireball Spell Hurls an explosive fireball dealing high fire damage.
2 Frost Nova Spell Freezes surrounding monsters and slows them down.
3 Chain Lightning Unleashes a electric bolt hitting multiple targets.
4 Aether Barrier Shield Absorbs incoming enemy damage with a light shield.
5 Use Health Potion Instantly restores 150 Health Points.
I Inventory Window Manage items, weapons, armors, and potions.
K Skill Matrix View skill trees and spend points to upgrade spells.
Q Quest Chronicle Track current active quests and claim rewards.
E Level Editor Studio Paint dungeon tiles, place monsters, export maps.
ESC Close Windows Closes any active modal window.

⚔️ How To Play & Progression

  1. Explore & Fight: Navigate the dungeon to encounter monsters (Skeleton Warriors 💀, Goblin Berserkers 👺, Shadow Liches 🧙‍♂️).
  2. Gain XP & Level Up: Defeating monsters rewards XP & Gold. Each Level Up restores HP/MP and boosts your Stats.
  3. Loot & Inventory: Open Inventory (I) to equip weapons/armors or use health potions.
  4. In-Game Level Editor: Press E to paint tiles or spawn extra monsters anywhere on the map!
